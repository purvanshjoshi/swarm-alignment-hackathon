The literature library used to prepare this project. The PDFs and saved web pages themselves are **not** included in this repository (they belong to their authors and publishers); everything below can be downloaded from the original links, or re-fetched with `download_papers.py`.

Items marked with a reference number are cited in the submission report (`docs/report/Purvansh_Joshi_Apart_Submission.pdf`). The rest were background reading.
