# Code of Conduct

This project adapts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility for our mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the community and for the research, not just for ourselves

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

Because this is AI-safety research, we also ask contributors not to use this project, its issues or its pull requests to share working attacks against real deployed systems, credentials, or other hazardous material. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for how to report such issues privately.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. This includes removing, editing or rejecting comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (the repository, its issues, discussions and pull requests) and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, [@purvanshjoshi](https://github.com/purvanshjoshi), through GitHub. Please do not report conduct issues in a public issue. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the reporter will be respected.

## Enforcement guidelines

1. **Correction.** For inappropriate language or other unprofessional behaviour: a private, written warning explaining the violation, and possibly a request for a public apology.
2. **Warning.** For a single serious incident or a series of actions: a warning with consequences for continued behaviour, including no interaction with the people involved for a specified period.
3. **Temporary ban.** For a serious violation, including sustained inappropriate behaviour: a temporary ban from any interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of groups of people: a permanent ban from any interaction with the project.
